Adding a photo: name it `banner-NN.jpg` (about 1600×560) or `quote-NN.jpg`
(about 900×400), use only public-domain or CC0 images, and add a row here.
Home shows one per day in turn. Your own banner: `~/.config/jarvis/banner.jpg`.
